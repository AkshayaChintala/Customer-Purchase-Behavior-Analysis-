# Customer-Purchase-Behavior-Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Customer Purchase Behavior data using Microsoft Excel

## Problem Statement
In the retail fashion industry, understanding customer purchasing behavior is essential for improving sales, customer satisfaction, and business performance. However, identifying purchasing patterns across different customer demographics, product categories, seasons, payment methods, and promotional offers can be challenging without proper data analysis.

## Objectives
- Analyze Customer Behavior
- Identify Top-Performing Categories
- Evaluate Customer Spending
- Analyze Seasonal Trends
- Study Customer Satisfaction
- Understand Payment and Shipping Preferences

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Formulas
- Slicers

## Key Insights
- Clothing is the top-performing category
- Credit card payments generate the highest revenue among payment methods.
- Free Shipping generates the highest revenue among shipping types
- Fall records the highest revenue among the seasons
- Adult customers (ages 26–50) account for the highest revenue.

## Recommendations
- Prioritize inventory planning for Clothing and Accessories because they generate the most revenue.
- Review seasonal sales patterns and plan marketing campaigns ahead of Fall.
- Investigate lower-rated products to identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.
- Evaluate discounts using conversion rates and profit margins, not revenue alone.
